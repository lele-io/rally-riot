# Rally Riot

Fast, chaotic badminton × tennis in the browser — 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3. Shuttle-and-net rallies with tennis power and spin.

**Play:** https://lele-io.github.io/rally-riot/

Controls: **WASD** move (camera-relative) · **Mouse** looks and aims (crosshair) · **Left click** swing (hold to charge a power swing) · **Space** jump · **Shift** dash (farther in mid-air) · **Wheel** zoom · **Hold Alt** to free the cursor.

Customize your character (hair, outfit, hat, face, colors) from the title screen.

Built with Three.js, Rapier and Vite. Models are made in Blender (`blender/build_models.py`).
